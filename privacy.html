Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use FixItNow, we collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the details of the service request you are posting (including your address).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to connect you with local service professionals, process your requests, send you quotes, and communicate with you about your jobs. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

When you submit a job request, we share the details of that request (including your name, contact info, and job address) with local professionals who match your criteria so they can provide you with quotes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@fixitnow-demo.com.
